What this version does and does not do

Solar position follows Meeus (Astronomical Algorithms) with nutation and aberration, checked against the PyEphem library to within a few seconds. Refraction uses the Saemundsson formula scaled by the pressure and temperature you enter, applied to the chosen point on the disk, not to the center.

The observation point and its skyline come from the Copernicus 30 m surface model, traced from 16 points on the hilltop east of Ramat Shlomo. Each date uses the point where the first sliver appears earliest. The map and schedule page explains the choice. The model includes buildings and trees and still needs a check on the ground, so treat times as good to about a minute.

Not yet in this version: a temperature profile or inversion model (the "Refraction multiplier" field lets you explore that by hand). That is Phase 3.

Times are rounded to the second but the real uncertainty on any given morning is tens of seconds from the atmosphere alone, which is exactly what the later phases and the calibration log are for.

Place and time

The coordinates fill themselves in with the point in the neighborhood where the first sliver is seen earliest on this date. Typing your own coordinates switches to a level horizon, because the terrain skyline is only stored for the 16 schedule points.

What counts as sunrise

Horizon

Skyline elevation is the angle of the ridge or building the Sun actually rises behind, measured from level. For a schedule point it is filled in from terrain data at the azimuth where the Sun appears; typing a number here replaces it. Observer height matters when the ground falls away toward the east; leave it at 0 if the skyline is above eye level.

Atmosphere

Using typical Jerusalem values. Press the button to pull live temperature and pressure.

Station pressure is the pressure where you stand, not the sea-level value weather sites show. Jerusalem at 780 m sits near 925 hPa. The multiplier is a hand control for the inversion question: a strong morning inversion over the rift bends light more (try 1.2 to 1.5), dry warm air less.
